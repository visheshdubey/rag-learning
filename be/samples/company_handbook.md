# Northwind Labs employee handbook

## Time off

Full-time employees receive 22 days of paid leave each calendar year.
Unused leave may be carried over up to 5 days into the next year.
Leave requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least 7 days in advance.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 local time.
A laptop and a stable internet connection are required on remote days.

## Expenses

Meals during client travel are reimbursed up to 45 USD per day.
Local commute is not reimbursable.
Submit receipts in Concur within 14 days of the expense.
